01

Three Main Responsibilities

  1. Design and develop spacecraft, rockets, and satellites.
  2. Test space technologies and solve engineering problems.
  3. Apply mathematics, physics, and engineering principles to space exploration.

Employment Sector & Workplaces

  • Government and private aerospace industries.
  • NASA, SpaceX, and the European Space Agency (ESA).
  • Research and development organizations.

Working Environment & Career Growth

Astronautical Engineers work with scientists and other engineers in teams. Career growth comes through experience, advanced education, and continuous improvement of technical skills.

02
Factor 1: Personal Goals

My childhood fascination with astronomy and space exploration motivates me to pursue this career. I will stay committed by continuously learning and improving my skills.

Factor 2: Qualifications

This career requires strong knowledge of mathematics, physics, programming, and engineering. I will maintain good academic performance and pursue the necessary college degree.

Factor 3: Job Availability

Opportunities in this field are limited and competitive. I will develop different technical skills, gain experience, and remain open to opportunities in the wider aerospace industry.

03

Main Reason

I want to turn my childhood fascination with space into something meaningful by helping develop technologies that make space exploration possible.

Possible Challenge

The high cost of education and specialized training, along with limited job opportunities in the space industry.

My Proposed Solution

I will maintain good grades, seek scholarships, and improve my programming, mathematics, physics, and engineering skills through courses and personal projects.

Alternative Career Plan (Plan B)

If I cannot pursue Astronautical Engineering immediately, I will consider Mechanical Engineering or Electrical Engineering. Both can lead to opportunities in advanced technology and aerospace.

04

1SHS Graduation Exit Pathway

Pursue higher education and build a strong foundation in mathematics, physics, and science.

2Education or Skills Training

Earn a Bachelor’s Degree in Aerospace Engineering, preferably specializing in Astronautical Engineering. Develop programming, electronics, and technical design skills.

3First Job / Entry Point

Work as a junior aerospace or spacecraft engineer in an aerospace company or research organization to gain practical experience.

4Long-Term Career Goal

Within 5–10 years, become an experienced Astronautical Engineer who contributes to developing spacecraft, satellites, and other space technologies.
